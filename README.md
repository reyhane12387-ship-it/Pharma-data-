# Pharmaceutical Sales Performance Analysis (Excel)

An Excel-based analysis of pharmaceutical sales covering revenue trends, product performance, sales team results, and regional insights.

- **Sales trends:** monthly revenue and seasonality
- **Products:** top-performing products and their revenue share
- **Regions:** revenue contribution by country/region
- **Sales team:** representative performance and ranking
- **KPI dashboard:** total revenue, average order value, product market share
