# Pharmaceutical Sales Data Analysis - Excel Project

## 📊 Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive sales performance analysis for pharmaceutical products using Microsoft Excel. The analysis covers revenue tracking, product performance, sales team evaluation, and regional market insights.

---

## 🎯 Key Analyses Performed

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis
- Total revenue and sales volume tracking
- Monthly sales trends and seasonality detection
- Year-over-year growth analysis

### 2. Product-Level Analysis
- Sales breakdown by individual pharmaceutical product
- Top-performing products identification
- Product category profitability comparison
- Visualized through charts and pivot tables

### 3. Regional Sales Analysis
- Sales performance across selected countries
- Market share comparison by region
- Country-wise revenue contribution

### 4. Sales Team Performance
- Individual sales representative performance tracking
- Ranking and KPI comparison
- Productivity and efficiency metrics

### 5. KPI Dashboard
- **Key Performance Indicators created:**
  - Total Revenue
  - Average Order Value
  - Sales Growth Rate
  - Product-wise Market Share
  - Regional Penetration Rate
  - Sales Rep Achievement Rate

### 6. Monthly Sales Range Analysis
- Sales distribution across different months
- Peak sales period identification
- Low-performance months analysis
- Seasonal pattern recognition
